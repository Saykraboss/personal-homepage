Comments and Conclusions

What I Found:

  • Most people (16 out of 20) buy things online. This shows that online shopping is really popular.
  • 8 people shop online once a month. This is the most common answer.
  • Most of the people I asked are between 18-25 years old. Young people use the internet more for shopping.
  • Amazon is the favorite site for 8 people. It is the most popular online store.
  • People with college education shop online more than others.

My Conclusion:

E-commerce is very popular today. Young people shop online often, and Amazon is the most used website. Most people buy things online at least once a month. Online shopping is easy and convenient, so more people prefer it.
