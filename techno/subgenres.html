Sonic Mutations

Acid Techno

135 - 142 BPM

Defined by the squelching, resonant, and psychedelic sounds of the Roland TB-303 synthesizer. It emerged in the late 1980s and became the raw, hypnotic backbone of illegal warehouse raves across the UK and Europe.

TB-303 Hypnotic Resonant

Minimal Techno

120 - 128 BPM

A stripped-back, skeletonized version of techno developed in the 1990s by pioneers like Robert Hood and Richie Hawtin. It focuses on subtle repetition, micro-glitches, and the absolute essence of the drum machine loop.

Sparsity Subtle Micro-Loops

Industrial Techno

130 - 140 BPM

Fusing the bleak, mechanical textures of industrial music with the driving rhythms of techno. It features heavy, distorted kicks, metallic percussions, and dark, dystopian avant-garde soundscapes.

Distorted Metallic Dystopian

Hard Techno

145 - 160+ BPM

An aggressive, high-speed variation that has dominated the modern scene. Characterized by relentless 4/4 pounding warehouse beats, distorted industrial synth stabs, and extreme, high-energy club environments.

High BPM Aggressive Relentless
