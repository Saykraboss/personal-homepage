SACRED SPACES

BERGHAIN

Berlin, Germany

Housed in a massive, former East German power plant, Berghain is widely considered the world capital of techno. Famous for its uncompromising industrial architecture, peerless sound system, strict no-photo policy, and legendary door selection.

Power Plant Strict Door No Photos

TRESOR

Berlin, Germany

Founded in 1991 in a defunct bank vault beneath East Berlin, Tresor is a historic pillar that bridged Detroit and Berlin techno. Today, located in a renovated power station (Kraftwerk), it remains a symbol of sonic freedom and underground rave culture.

Vault Roots Kraftwerk Berlin Blueprint

FABRIC

London, UK

A cornerstone of British club culture since 1999. Located in a former cold storage warehouse in Farringdon, Fabric is world-renowned for its legendary "bodysonic" dancefloor (Room 1) that transmits bass frequencies directly through the floorboards.

Warehouse Bodysonic Floor UK Clubbing

SHELTER

Amsterdam, Netherlands

Located directly beneath the Overhoeks Tower across the IJ river, Shelter offers a literal subterranean haven. Designed as an industrial concrete bunker, it pairs a minimalistic aesthetic with a top-tier immersive sound system built for extended dark sessions.
