Chapter 01

The Roots of Rhythm

1980s

The Detroit Genesis

Born in the industrial landscape of Detroit, Michigan. High school friends known as the "Belleville Three" fused the futuristic synth-pop of Kraftwerk with Afro-American funk and jazz, creating a new, soulful machine music designed for urban survival.

1991

The Berlin Alliance

Following the fall of the Berlin Wall, empty power plants, bunkers, and underground vaults in East Germany became the concrete canvas for a reunified youth. Techno became the soundtrack of freedom, raw expression, and historical reconciliation.

NOW

The Global Movement

What started in small, dark warehouses has exploded into a multi-billion dollar global subculture. From massive festival mainstages to intimate underground basements, techno remains a worldwide language of unity, rhythm, and sonic exploration.
