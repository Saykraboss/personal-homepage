Chapter 05

MACHINES OF RHYTHM

Drum Machine

Roland TR-909

The definitive backbone of techno music. Released in 1983, this hybrid analog-digital drum machine introduced the world to the deep, punchy analog kick drum and crisp sample-based hi-hats that continue to define the genre's driving energy today.

Year of Release 1983
Signature Sound The 909 Kick & Open Hat
Bass Synthesizer

Roland TB-303

Originally designed as a commercial failure to replace bass guitarists, the 303 was adopted by underground producers who turned its resonance knobs to the extreme. This accidental abuse birthed the squelching, hypnotic, and psychedelic "Acid" basslines.

Year of Release 1981
Signature Sound Squelchy, Resonant Acid Bass
Turntable

Technics SL-1210

The tank of the DJ world. With its legendary high-torque direct-drive motor and indestructible build quality, the Technics SL series allowed DJs to beatmatch with extreme precision amidst the heavy vibrations of smoke-filled underground warehouses.

Drive Type Direct Drive (High Torque)
Industry Standard Vinyl Club Performance
DJ Mixer

Pioneer DJM-900

The modern command center. As digital setups overtook traditional setups, this 4-channel digital mixer became the global festival standard, allowing artists to shape sound with surgical EQing, crisp isolators, and complex built-in audio effects.

Channels 4-Channel Digital
Key Feature Sound Color FX & Beat FX
